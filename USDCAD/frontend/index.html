06 / MODEL & DATA

A model you can trace

The selected specification

Rolling 756-business-day OLS of log USD/CAD on the US–Canada 2Y spread, log WTI, VIX and log broad USD. Refit at business year-end; coefficients stay fixed between refits.

The BEER residual is re-centered and scaled over 252 business days. PPP shifts its tier outward on agreement, inward on opposition. Layer 2 grades the mean of three CAD cross z-scores. Each layer maps seven tiers to USD weights of 0%, 20%, 35%, 50%, 65%, 80%, 100%; the portfolio averages their weights.

Valuation context, with limits

The case study reports an information ratio of 0.50 for 2011–2026, falling to 0.04 from 2019 onward. After 261 tested variants, the deflated significance measure is 0.32.

Use this as a valuation tilt. PPP is a long-run anchor; the baseline does not apply the optional VIX stress filter.

These are the paper's reported results, not a new live backtest. Daily public fixings are not executable intraday quotes.
